# Charmlet website direction

**A little company for your big ideas.** Charmlet brings expressive digital companions, quiet focus tools and a personal collection to the places people already work.

## Brand

- Audience: developers, creators and people who spend their day at a computer.
- Personality: warm, curious, playful and quietly capable. Cali is the leading character.
- Promise: make a workspace feel personal, with encouragement and small moments of play.
- Product language: digital companions for desktop and VS Code. Say this in the first screen so visitors do not mistake the charms for physical products.
- Current offer: the free trial and its complete included collection. Extra paid digital collections are a future, separately configured offer.
- Physical merchandise: a later shop category. No merchandise purchase buttons or shipping promises at this stage.

## Visual system

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Typography | Self-hosted Manrope, strong display headings and readable body text |
| Main colours | Warm cloud `#f7f8f3`, deep ink `#19362e`, soft lime `#e2efa5` |
| Supporting colours | Pale blue, warm peach and lavender, used as artwork backdrops |
| Artwork | The approved Charmlet figures and real app captures |
| Layout | Generous spacing, fewer competing calls to action and strong section hierarchy |
| Motion | Real interactions and restrained section reveals; respect Reduced Motion and stop work offscreen |
| Voice | Short, specific and encouraging. No fabricated testimonials, popularity claims or scarcity |

Manrope was retrieved from the Google Fonts repository on 2026-11-10. Its SIL Open Font License is preserved alongside the font in `website/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Font SHA256: `3ae11c49db0455a3cc33e37d380f20fdb8c7f8b41dc07625c177e3d87a9d6ae6`.

## Page structure

1. **Homepage:** a large live Cali playground, clear download and exploration actions, a concise product story, featured collections, garden and focus benefits, installation and FAQs.
2. **Charm gallery:** a dedicated searchable collection, filters, clear included status and an on-demand interactive preview. Keep the visitor near their chosen charm instead of jumping to the top of a long page.
3. **Artwork review:** retain the detailed owner-review page and historical comparisons, linked discreetly.
4. **Hosted shop, later:** a separate destination for approved new digital products; physical merchandise can join the same shop when ready.

Install controls must reflect real availability. A local Windows archive does not establish a public download URL. Extension-store, desktop-release and shop links appear only when valid destinations are configured. Keep older pack URLs working.

## Reference video

Reference supplied by the owner: [Create a Pro Website — How to Build $10,000 Websites with Claude Code](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQdmqxY1RaI). The English transcript was read through YouTube's public transcript panel.

| Video section | Application to Charmlet |
|---|---|
| 4:29 — brand kit | Establish the audience, personality, typography and palette before changing pages |
| 7:30 — reference imagery | Use a coherent set of approved figures and real product assets |
| 9:24 — hero motion | Let visitors experience the actual live companion and its reactions |
| 11:21 — design instructions | Record layout, interaction and accessibility rules in this document |
| 12:55–18:42 — build and refine | Run the site, inspect real screens, refine spacing and test every action |

The tutorial uses a coffee brand, generated video and a sponsored hosting flow. Charmlet's implementation uses its own identity, existing live renderer and the owner's Vercel direction. The video's example checkout is initially unconnected; Charmlet's purchase links must lead to a configured store with tested delivery before they are exposed.

## Validation

Check both pages on desktop and mobile, including narrow screens, keyboard navigation, dialog focus, Reduced Motion, image loading and idle rendering. Verify downloads against the packaged app, and verify active shop/download links separately from their visual appearance.
