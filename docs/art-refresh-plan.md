# Artwork refresh — November 2026 review

Use recognizable objects, convincing materials and distinct silhouettes. A design must make sense at the app's actual charm size without depending on its label. The collection does not need to meet a numerical quota.

- More Bay Area and Silicon Valley subjects, using recognizable places and objects.

The owner additionally requested a strictly motivational quote feed: direct encouragement toward focus, discipline, progress and success. Replace casual jokes and generic good wishes with original, positive, action-oriented messages. Preserve the existing message identities and remove no saved progress.

## Implementation checkpoint — 2026-11-03

The 0.5.2 source contains the motivational deck, soft squeeze interaction and optional festive defaults. The refresh prepared seventeen new or revised candidates with Gemini 3 Pro Image: the seven requested replacements, two Stress Busters, Kovil, Cricket, Robot Assistant and five seasonal designs. Curation shows 64 designs, including the original Terminal and twelve earned flowers; 51 are individually importable. Sixteen packs include a nine-design Refresh Sample, Bay Area Days and Seasonal. Archived files and old download identities remain intact.

Festive selection is off initially. It derives a visible choice from the local date and installed artwork without overwriting the usual selection. A manual choice, import or explicit pin protects that charm across dates and reloads; older saved choices migrate as pinned. Reset position retains these preferences. This is stronger than a one-day override.

Local validation passed 66 unit tests and nine Windows VS Code 1.140 scenarios, including the installed package. The main and review website checks passed. Twenty-five actual installed-app captures at 100% are summarized in the [review sheet](art-refresh-review.md), including light backgrounds. The Windows desktop 0.1.2 archive was extracted and tested. Hosted checks are tracked in the PR.

Owner feedback remains required. Named deity, lamp and Pongal concepts stay on the idea list; named athlete and league artwork remains behind the rights gate. Final bundled/website/paid placement, licences and public launch approval remain separate decisions.
