## Art direction and delivery

- Keep the recognizable Charmlet silhouette, hook and restrained mint/graphite/copper palette; use brighter accents for AI and nature collections.
- Design for the small charm surface first: readable silhouettes, strong contrast, a few purposeful details and no tiny text.
- Make each design visually distinct. A target count does not justify many indistinguishable color swaps.
- Keep the existing safe static PNG pack payload. Source SVGs are trusted build inputs, not arbitrary uploaded executable content.
- New work ships in reviewed batches. Each source records author, origin, references and intended distribution license; no unsupported “license-free” label.
- Preserve existing individual downloads and pack IDs. Any later bundle must avoid confusing duplicate installs and stay within the current parser's per-pack limits.
- Optional paid collections must be additional original artwork; never move a promised free item behind payment.

## Paid personal-photo direction

The website Photo Studio offers calm keepsakes: a small portrait locket, pet silhouette, family-photo print or simple frame. The paid part is the creation tool/service; the exported pack uses the ordinary free importer and works offline.

Prefer local crop/fit/background preview and metadata-free static exports. No automatic photo sharing, public gallery entry or retained upload is implied. Advanced generative styling is a later proposal with its own cost/privacy/rights review.

Exact premium-pack and Studio prices remain undecided. Marvel/DC, recognizable licensed characters and company-logo collections are Phase 7+ rights-dependent work.
