WHY A LITTLE COMPANION?

Work has room for personality.

A terminal can be a familiar face. A bear can cheer a finished task. A tiny garden can remind us to step away for a moment. We are building those small interactions into something you can keep beside your work.

The California collection draws from the places around us: the coast, redwoods, mountains and the everyday rhythm of the Bay Area.

HOW WE WANT TO BUILD IT

Useful before paid

The free edition should be worth keeping. Focus, your garden, personal photos and the starter companions remain useful on their own.

Personal stays personal

Your photo keepsakes and app progress stay on your device. A download request does not subscribe you to marketing.

Little things, done carefully

A cord that settles, a clear keyboard focus, a celebration you can turn off. We care about how the app feels in everyday use.

Grow at a sensible pace

Our focus is the app and digital charms. Physical merchandise is a future possibility; there is no merchandise store or preorder today.

Come hang out.
