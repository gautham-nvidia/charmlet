    Available now

    Install the extension file

    A .vsix file installs Charmlet in your editor.

    Download Charmlet .vsix ↓
    1. Download the free extension file above.
    2. In desktop VS Code, open the Command Palette: Ctrl + Shift + P on Windows/Linux, or Cmd + Shift + P on macOS.
    3. Run Extensions: Install from VSIX…, choose the downloaded .vsix file and follow your editor's confirmation prompts.
    4. Reload the editor window, then run Charmlet: Show Charm.
    After publication

    Store listings are being prepared. Until the official links appear here, use the extension file.

    1. Once published, use the official store link for your editor.
    2. Open the editor's Extensions view, find the verified Charmlet listing and choose Install.
    3. Run Charmlet: Show Charm. Store installation then provides the editor's normal extension update flow.

    A FEW SMALL ANSWERS

    Good to know.

    Is Charmlet free?

    Yes. The trial includes every charm, focus tools, small wins and local photo keepsakes. Optional paid collections and the separate website Photo Studio remain undecided and unavailable for purchase.

    Can I use my own photo?

    Yes. Open Charms > Make a photo charm or Settings > Photo charms. Choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP, adjust the crop, pick a frame and save. Everything happens on your device. Only the finished keepsake is stored; nothing is uploaded. You can remove it from the same panel.

    What does Sunny do?

    Sunny is an original live yellow smiley. It faces you, blinks, glances toward a nearby pointer and reacts when squeezed or when you record a win. It returns its gaze to the centre after a short pause. Reduced Motion keeps it still, with quiet expression changes.

    Can I change the cord?

    Yes. Charms choose a matching cord, such as a chain for a gemstone or a soft cord for a stress ball. Choose a different material in Settings > Appearance; each charm remembers its own choice.

    How do small wins work?

    Open Focus > Small wins, write a short achievement and add it. You can undo an entry or clear the history. A focus session that finishes while the app is running earns one win. Brief milestone celebrations are optional and off by default; Reduced Motion uses a still star.

    Can I try the refreshed artwork in the app?

    Yes. Install the current trial and open Charms. The complete selected collection is already there, including the new lab tools, all eight planets, gemstones, zodiac and sacred-tradition artwork. Existing imports and garden progress stay intact. The final release lineup and free or paid downloads will be chosen later.

    What about athlete and league collections?

    Sports has separate football, basketball, American football and baseball ideas. Named athlete and league editions are a wishlist, not current downloads or an endorsement. Their image and branding rights need a separate review.

    What do Focus and Learn include?

    Focus provides saved focus/break sessions with pause and resume. Motivation is the default: 80 original messages about effort, discipline, progress and success. Learn also offers 60 sourced facts and 30 trivia questions.

    How do festive defaults work?

    New profiles follow 21 US celebration dates automatically, with a changing Christmas collection through December. Updates preserve existing opt-outs and pins. Choose Settings → Appearance → Follow holiday defaults to enable and unpin in one click. Picking a charm pins it; you can switch festive defaults off at any time. The usual saved charm returns on other days.

    How does the garden work?

    Click Garden in the toolbar, then Plant a seed, or run Charmlet: Open Garden. Plant one unknown seed and water once per local day. Missing days do not hurt progress. The eleventh valid watering adds its original flower to Grown by you. Water and move reminders are optional and off by default.

    Do I need an account or an internet connection?

    No account is needed for the app. Once installed or imported, your charms, focus tools and garden work offline. AI is used to prepare the new artwork, not to run the extension.

    How do I install this preview?

    Use the current manual installation steps. Official extension-store links will appear there only after publication.

    Will imported charms survive updates?

    Yes. Imported packs are stored separately from the extension. You can remove them using Charmlet: Remove Charm Pack.

    What is Photo Studio?

    Photo Studio is a prepared optional service for turning a personal photo into a data-only Charmlet pack. Photo processing stays in the browser, and importing remains free. It is Coming Soon by default; no price or live purchase is offered here.

    Will there be paid packs?

    Optional artwork collections remain a proposal. The extension, its functionality, importing, starter charms and current review packs stay free. No paid pack is on sale in this preview.
