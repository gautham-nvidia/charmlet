A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Curious?
Good.

Is Charmlet free?

The current trial and its complete collection are free. Optional paid digital collections may come later; no payment is needed to try the app.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is needed. After installation, the included charms, focus tools, puzzles and garden work offline. Your preferences and progress are saved on your computer.

Can I make it bigger or keep things quiet?

Yes. Appearance supports sizes from 60% to 200%. You can change Cali's outfit, hide extra controls and turn encouragement and celebrations off. Reduced Motion keeps things still.

Where do I plant my first seed?

Use Open garden in the app, or run Charmlet: Open Garden in VS Code. Choose Plant a seed, then water once each local day. Eleven watering days earn a flower; missed days never remove progress.

How do optional charm packs work?

The complete trial collection comes with the app. If you have an extra .charmlet.json pack, run Charmlet: Import Charm Pack in VS Code or choose Import Pack… from the desktop tray. Image packs add artwork; live behaviour comes with the app.

Are these physical charms?

These are digital companions for your computer. Physical merchandise is planned for a later chapter.
