A LITTLE COMPANY FOR YOUR CHARM

One favourite.
Or a few, together.

Two optional layouts to consider for a later update. The current app keeps one charm, with a quiet desktop by default.

01 / Curved band

A small bracelet.

Three pieces share a curved support. This feels closest to a charm bracelet.

The band would stay in place while you play with a charm. Start with two or three pieces to leave room between them.

02 / Hanging row

A little line-up.

Each piece has its own peg and cord. More room to see and pull each one.

The clearest arrangement at small sizes. It could sit along a desktop edge, with each cord matched to its charm.

These are layout sketches, not screenshots of a working multi-charm mode. You can keep using one charm; a future layout choice would be optional.
