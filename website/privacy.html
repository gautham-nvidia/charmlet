Your download request

We ask only for your email to send a confirmation link through Brevo. No username or password is created. Cloudflare provides the website, verification service, protected file storage and a check against automated signup abuse.

Confirmation links expire after 15 minutes and work once. The address in a pending request is cleared when the link is used; expired requests are removed by scheduled cleanup. A short download session lasts up to one hour. Its random access token is kept in this browser tab's session storage so you can return from checkout.

Trials and purchases

The service retains a keyed, one-way identifier of your normalized email to remember your original trial deadline and your purchases. Reinstalling or requesting another email link does not reset the trial. Gmail dots and plus aliases count as the same address. There is no payment card or automatic charge for a trial.

When checkout opens, Lemon Squeezy will process payment, receipts and applicable transaction taxes. It receives the billing details you enter there; Charmlet does not receive your full card number. We keep purchase references, item ownership and refund status so your purchases can be restored.

Offers are optional

You can join through the Pro offers popup, or choose the separate, unchecked offers box when requesting a download. Either route asks you to confirm your email before joining the list. Requesting a download alone does not opt you into offers. Subscribing does not start your trial or unlock Pro. Offer emails will include an unsubscribe link, and you can keep using the app after unsubscribing.

Your photos and app

Photos you try in the website locket stay in the current browser tab. They are not uploaded or saved by this page. The site stores your colour-theme choice, basket and the time you dismissed the offers popup on this device. Dismissal pauses automatic offers for seven days; you can reopen them from the footer. Your email is not saved in browser storage. Your installed Charmlet settings, photos and progress stay in the app's local storage.

An activated app checks signed access with the service when connected. It sends the signed access record, not your photos, code or garden history. Purchase access files are personal; keep them out of public repositories.

Before signup opens

Our sender, contact address and provider accounts must be connected before real signup starts. Trial identifiers are retained to enforce one trial per email; purchase records are retained to honour your access. Contact us to ask about your records or deletion once the service opens.
