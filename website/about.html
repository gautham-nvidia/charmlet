WHERE IT LIVES

On your desktop

A small floating companion sits above your other apps. Place it where you like, open its controls from the tray, and let it keep you company without taking over your screen.

Inside VS Code

Open the Charmlet sidebar next to your work. Your companion, focus session, garden and little breaks stay together, without changing your code.

A LITTLE MORE THAN A PRETTY FACE

Focus on one thing

Set a focus session and give the next small task your attention. Pause when you need to; your timer and settings stay on your device.

Notice the small wins

Finished a tricky fix? Took a break? Log the moment. Your companion has a cheer, a little gesture and a burst of confetti ready.

Take a playful pause

Spin a fidget, try a puzzle, read some coding trivia or water your garden. There is no score to keep up with and no feed to scroll.

Make it yours

Choose a companion, change its cord or make a keepsake from your own photo. Photos and personal packs stay on your device.
